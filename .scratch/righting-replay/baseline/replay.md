## Policy decisions

### Included fields

- **Aliases:** `component`/Client, `route`/Client, and `library`/Utility are reusable directory vocabulary, not per-file maps. They directly reflect the repository layout and `AGENTS.md` role statements.
- **Composition roots:** `worker`, `router`, and `routeTree` represent integration/wiring, not business roles.
- **Guidance:** `CONTEXT.md` is the explicit domain vocabulary referenced by `AGENTS.md`.

### Variations — evaluated individually

| Variation | Disposition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `clientReadsAccess` | **omit** | One existing Client → ResourceAccess edge (`src/routes/index.tsx:9`) conflicts with the stated separation in `AGENTS.md`; a project-wide relaxation is not supported. |
| `pureEngines` | **omit** | Current engines have no direct ResourceAccess imports, but no maintainer decision commits all future engines to purity. |
| `contextFirewall` | **omit** | No bounded contexts/scopes are declared in project guidance. Adding scopes would invent architecture and require context/shared/unscoped patterns. |

### Overrides — evaluated

**No override proposed.** A Client → Client allowance would hide 32 UI-composition edges globally; Client → Engine, Client → ResourceAccess, ResourceAccess → Engine, Engine → Engine, and Manager → Client allowances would likewise turn current role leaks into permanent policy. Extraction or responsibility clarification is preferable to a global relaxation. No tightening override has evidence beyond the preset.

### Protected dependencies and golden examples

**Omit both.** `package.json` identifies dependencies but contains no maintainer classification of a third-party package as a protected Resource or Utility. `CONTEXT.md` is a vocabulary, not a named executable golden example.
